# NIKWAY Community Code of Conduct | منشور رفتار

We aim to build a respectful, inclusive and evidence-based engineering community.

## Expected behavior
- Be professional, constructive and considerate across languages and skill levels.
- Critique ideas and code, not people; provide reproducible evidence.
- Respect confidentiality, consent, licenses and attribution.
- Welcome newcomers and acknowledge differing perspectives.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, unwanted personal contact, disclosure of private information, intentional disruption, and attempts to access unauthorized systems.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit, hide or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation proportionately. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an established GitHub private channel; do not publish personal or sensitive details in public issues.

## Scope
Repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed community spaces.

## فارسی
در همکاری نیک‌وی، احترام، گفت‌وگوی مستند، رعایت حقوق دیگران و حفاظت از اطلاعات محرمانه الزامی است. توهین، آزار، تهدید، افشای اطلاعات خصوصی و دسترسی غیرمجاز پذیرفته نیست. موارد حساس را خصوصی به مسئول مخزن گزارش کنید.
