# NIKWAY vNx2 — Startup Failure Lessons & Governance Controls
Date: 2026-11-05
Status: Research hypotheses / proposed controls; NOT approved architecture changes or implemented controls.

## Purpose
Translate lessons from failed or challenged startups and projects into falsifiable NIKWAY design, contracting, delivery and governance checks. Separate verified company facts, attributed accounts, and NIKWAY hypotheses. Do not equate acquisition, restructuring or product closure with company failure.

## Critical gate: AI project feasibility BEFORE commitment
Before fixed-price delivery or full implementation commitments, perform paid or scoped discovery assessing customer data quality/access, integrations, security, model accuracy and evaluation, deployment constraints, AI inference and human review costs, customer responsibilities, timeline and measurable outcome. Specify acceptance tests, scope/change control, cost ceiling, support obligations, exit/export and no-go criteria. Track technical delivery, contract margin and realized customer benefit separately.

## Founder & Governance Risk
- Cofounder conflict: document ownership, decision rights, dispute resolution and vesting.
- Founder bottleneck/dependency: delegate authority; document knowledge, architecture decisions, customer relationships and operating procedures.
- Confirmation bias: independent customer discovery and counterevidence review.
- Premature scaling: stage hiring, services, regions and spend behind validated demand.
- Burnout: sustainable operating cadence, backups and escalation paths.
- Skills gaps: cover sales, finance, product, delivery and security with accountable owners.
- Failure to delegate: explicit RACI/decision authority and deputies.
- Sunk-cost persistence: pre-agreed stop/pivot criteria and periodic portfolio review.
Controls: ADR/decision log, delegation matrix, succession coverage, stop rules, independent review.

## Domain-specific research and candidate controls
| Area | Failure mode to test | Proposed safeguard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B2B SaaS | product built without paying demand | customer interviews, paid pilots, unit economics |
| Knowledge/RAG | document search used without measurable benefit | source-grounded evaluation, task-time/quality outcome |
| AI agents | demo works but production data/integrations fail | feasibility gate, eval set, human approval, rollback |
| AI infrastructure | inference/storage/support cost exceeds revenue | per-tenant/service cost metering, model portability |
| Consulting | human delivery hidden in software margins | explicit human/AI service boundaries and project costing |
| Improvement/Lean Six Sigma | tools taught but not applied | real improvement projects, verified benefits |
| Corporate learning | completion without skill transfer | practical assessment and 30/90-day follow-up |
| Workshops/bootcamps | low occupancy or overpromised outcomes | break-even threshold, truthful claims, outcome evidence |
| Physical training kits | inventory/printing/shipping costs | digital master, small batches/print-on-demand |
| Gamification | point farming and shallow engagement | reward verified learning/action, not raw clicks |
| Adaptive UI | unpredictable changes cause errors | explainable, reversible, user-controlled adaptation |
| Badges/certification | badges lack evidence or portability | separate attendance/learning/skill/certification; consider Open Badges 3.0 |
| Internationalization | locale confused with jurisdiction | stable concept IDs, localized labels, legal review, export/restore |
| Distributed services | integrations and ops costs balloon | ownership, service cost budgets, failure/exit tests |

## Candidate evidence register (verify primary sources before adoption)
- Kite: AI coding assistant shutdown; investigate quality and monetization.
- Neeva: consumer search product shutdown; distinguish product closure from acquisition.
- Relay.app: workflow platform closure; verify claims about integration maintenance and economics against original statements.
- Transpose: information management closure; investigate revenue/positioning.
- Teamometer: team improvement product; investigate founder interview and willingness to pay.
- FrontRow, Dev Bootcamp, The Iron Yard, 2U: learning and bootcamp business models; distinguish closure vs restructuring.
- Kukucrate, Heathkit Educational Systems, Rafter: physical educational kits/materials and distribution.
- Badgeville and Credly: not proof of failure; examine credential/gamification business models.
- MoreSteam and KaiNexus: active positive benchmarks for training + tools + improvement execution.
- Builder.ai, ScaleFactor: investigate documented claims about human labor, automation, finances; avoid unverified attribution.

## Decision process
1. For each case, capture dated primary evidence, company status, business model, causal claim and confidence.
2. Map to NIKWAY risk, existing control, severity, owner and candidate acceptance test.
3. Prototype and test with synthetic data or paid pilots.
4. Approve changes via ADR/review; only then modify implementation.
5. Record outcomes and revisit at defined checkpoints.

## Initial proposed acceptance checks
- No AI delivery commitment without feasibility report and signed scope/acceptance criteria.
- No new integration without owner, cost estimate, test and export/fallback strategy.
- No paid workshop without break-even model and measurable learning/transfer objectives.
- No credential labeled professional competence without assessment evidence and issuer rules.
- No country node launch without localization/legal review and independent restore/export drill.
