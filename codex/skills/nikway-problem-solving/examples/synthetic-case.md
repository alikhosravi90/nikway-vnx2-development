# Synthetic demonstration — delayed order fulfillment

**Synthetic / not a real customer case.**

Reported: Orders are sometimes shipped late. No baseline or target is provided.

Observed: None.

Unknown: late-order percentage, time period, fulfillment stages, owners, customer impact.

Hypothesis: Handoffs between picking and packing may cause delays. Not verified.

A3 problem statement: The frequency and cause of late shipments are not yet established. First define the promised shipment date and collect a representative baseline.

5 Whys: Do not assert a root cause. Start with the question "Which stage contributes most to the observed delay?" and request timestamp evidence.

PDCA proposal: For two weeks, measure timestamps at order receipt, picking, packing and dispatch. Review distribution and missing data before choosing countermeasures.

Approval gate: No operational process changes without owner approval.
